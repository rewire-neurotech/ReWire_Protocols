Important

ReWire turns your words into a short, intense voice-over-music experience. It's generated automatically, so it can be inaccurate, off-base, or upsetting. Use it somewhere private, never while driving, and stop any time.

If you're in crisis or having thoughts of harming yourself, please don't use this now. Call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number. If you're being treated for a mental health condition, check with your clinician before using ReWire.
