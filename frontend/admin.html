What happens when something gets flagged

Read this once and the tab below makes sense.

1 · instant

Screening

Someone writes a goal. ST1 reads it before a single word is generated.

2 · instant, automatic

The app responds by itself

If it's flagged, generation stops and crisis resources appear straight away. No human is involved and nobody is waiting on one.

3 · later, you

You check the call

It lands in the list below. You read what they wrote and say whether the screen got it right.

The list below is quality control, not an emergency room. The person was already helped, in the moment, by step 2. Reading a case two days later changes nothing for them — it changes whether ST1 is tuned correctly for the next person. That's why there's no stopwatch on it, and why the only thing being asked of you is right or wrong.

Was the screen right?

What the person actually wrote, next to what the app did about it. Your answer is the only thing that tells you whether screening is tuned right, so a wrong call is as useful to record as a right one.
